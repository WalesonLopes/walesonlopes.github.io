Who I Am

I’m based in São Paulo, Brazil, with a Bachelor’s degree in Portuguese Language and Linguistics from the University of São Paulo (USP). For the past decade, I’ve worked as a translator and linguistic reviewer for European language service providers, handling English and German as my main source languages into Brazilian Portuguese.

This experience trained me to read data very carefully: spotting inconsistencies, applying guidelines, checking terminology, and ensuring that every detail aligns with context and audience. Over time, this naturally evolved into work with AI-generated content, data annotation, and quality evaluation for LLM outputs.

Data & Analytics Background

To formalize my interest in data, I completed the Data Analytics Bootcamp (Excel, SQL, Power BI, Tableau) with Alex The Analyst, working on hands-on projects focused on:

  • Data cleaning and exploratory analysis in MySQL (joins, subqueries, window functions, CTEs).
  • Building reports and dashboards in Excel (formulas, pivot tables, XLOOKUP, charts, conditional formatting).
  • Interactive visualizations in Tableau.
  • End-to-end dashboards in Power BI (Power Query, data modeling, DAX, drill-down, KPIs).

My goal now is to combine this analytical foundation with my language and AI background, working in roles that involve data analysis, monitoring, and insight generation, especially where AI and language data intersect.
